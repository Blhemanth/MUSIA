HOW MUSIA QUANTUM DIFFUSION WORKS

1 Multilingual Narrative Parser

Parses prose in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, and Bengali. Detects punctuation including Latin periods and Indic Purna Viram (।) into clean, sequential visual cues.

2 4-Qubit PQC Hilbert Modulation

PennyLane compresses CLIP tokens into a 4-wire quantum register using AngleEmbedding and BasicEntanglerLayers, computing Pauli-Z expectations to enrich prompt latents.

3 SDXL LoRA Diffusion Bridge

Modulated latents are synthesized via Google Colab GPU (or the Instant Procedural Preview fallback) applying custom LoRA cinematic film weights.

4 Director's Cut & Master Export

Inspect frames through WebGL fragment shaders, execute side-by-side / split slider quantum comparisons, and export as ZIP package, JSON metadata, or printable PDF.
